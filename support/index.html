What Chill Player does

Chill Player plays the movies and shows already in your own put.io cloud storage, on your own Jellyfin servers and on your Plex account's servers, matched to artwork, cast and episodes, with resume, subtitles and offline downloads. You can browse movies and shows, see where they stream and try the demo without an account. It can't search for, download or add content from the internet.

Common questions

  • How do I sign in? Tap “Sign in with put.io” and sign in on put.io's own page; the app never sees your password. On Apple TV, enter the code shown at put.io/link on your phone or computer.
  • How do I add my Jellyfin server? Go to Settings → Sources → Add Jellyfin Server, enter the server's address, then use Quick Connect or your password. Servers outside your home network need https.
  • How do I add Plex? Go to Settings → Sources → Add Plex Account. The app shows a code: enter it at plex.tv/link on your phone or computer, and your Plex servers appear under Sources.
  • Can I try it without an account? Yes: tap “Try the Demo” on Home or Library. It uses sample public-domain films. Settings → Leave Demo goes back.
  • A video is matched to the wrong movie or show. Open it and choose Fix Match.
  • Subtitle search doesn't work. Search uses OpenSubtitles with your own free API key: add it in Settings → Subtitles. Subtitles that come with your files work without a key.
  • Where do the extra ratings come from? From MDBList, with your own free key in Settings → Connected Services.
  • Where does “Where to Watch” come from? Streaming availability from JustWatch, through TMDB, for your device's country.
  • How do I sign out or remove my data? Settings → Sign Out. Deleting the app removes everything it kept on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
