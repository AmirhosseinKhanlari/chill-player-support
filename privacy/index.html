Chill Player Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

In short: Chill Player has no accounts of its own, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We (the developer) don't receive, see or sell any of your data. The app talks directly to the services listed below, only to do what you ask it to do, and keeps everything else on your device.

What Chill Player is

Chill Player is a player and library manager for your own videos: the ones in your put.io cloud storage and, if you add them, on your own Jellyfin servers. It shows the movies and shows already there with artwork and details, and plays them. You can also browse movies and shows without signing in to anything. It can't search for, download or add content from the internet. Chill Player is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by put.io or any other service named here.

What leaves your device, and to whom

The app sends requests straight from your device to these services, only when you use the feature that needs them, and only what that feature needs. Each service receives that data only to provide the feature to you, and handles it under its own privacy policy, which gives your data the same or equal protection as described in this policy. We (the developer) never receive your data, so we don't share it with anyone.

TMDB (The Movie Database) What you search for (including Ask Chill searches), the titles you open or follow, titles and years read from your put.io file names (and, for Jellyfin, the TMDB ids your server already has) (to match them to movies and shows), your device's region (for "New to Stream"), and requests for posters and images. OpenSubtitles (only if you add your own OpenSubtitles API key) Your OpenSubtitles API key and, when you search for subtitles: the title (its TMDB id, or a name read from the file), season and episode, the language you want, and a short fingerprint ("hash") of the video file. On Apple TV, Trailer opens the video in the YouTube app instead. Playing trailers. "Watch on YouTube" opens the YouTube app or website.

In the demo ("Try the Demo"), the sample titles are public-domain films and Creative Commons shorts, sample videos stream from Apple's public test streams, and no put.io account is used.

Apple features

  • Notifications for new episodes are asked for only when you turn on an alert, and are scheduled on your device (local notifications). There are no push notifications and no notification server.
  • Ask Chill turns your sentence into search filters with Apple Intelligence's on-device model (on supported devices). The sentence isn't sent to us; the resulting search goes to TMDB like any other search.
  • Spotlight, Siri and widgets use titles from your library that the app saves on your device. On Apple TV, the Top Shelf shows your Continue Watching and recently added titles the same way.
  • iCloud Keychain: your service keys (MDBList, OpenSubtitles), OpenSubtitles sign-in and Trakt username are saved in the Keychain and, if you use iCloud Keychain, sync to your other Apple devices (end-to-end encrypted by Apple). Your put.io token stays on the device where you signed in.

How long data is kept

  • We keep nothing on servers: the app has no servers, so there is nothing of yours for us to store or delete.
  • Data on your device stays there until you sign out or remove it in Settings, or delete the app (see below).
  • Data you send to put.io, your Jellyfin server, TMDB, Trakt, MDBList, OpenSubtitles or YouTube is kept by that service as described in its own privacy policy.

What stays on your device

  • Sign-in tokens and keys, in the Keychain. Your put.io token and Jellyfin access keys stay on this device and aren't synced.
  • Settings, the addresses and names of your Jellyfin servers, My List, followed shows, your library's matches to TMDB, recent searches, subtitle choices, a ratings cache and saved subtitle files.
  • Offline downloads of videos you choose to download. Videos from Jellyfin servers aren't downloaded or cached on the device.
  • A small shared container (App Group) used only by the app and its own widget (on Apple TV, its Top Shelf): Continue Watching titles, their pictures and a pending link from a control.

What we don't do

  • No accounts of our own, and no servers of our own: nothing is sent to the developer.
  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your data, because we never have it.

Children

Chill Player is not directed to children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

  • Sign out of put.io in Settings to remove the put.io token from the device.
  • In Settings → Sources, remove a Jellyfin server to delete its access key from the device and sign this device out on the server, and to drop its titles from your library.
  • In Settings → Connected Services you can disconnect Trakt and remove your MDBList key; in Settings → Subtitles you can remove your OpenSubtitles key and sign out of OpenSubtitles.
  • Delete the app to remove its settings, library data, caches, downloads and the widget's shared data. Keychain items can outlive the app on iOS, so remove keys in Settings first if you want them gone.
  • To cut off the app's access to put.io from anywhere, revoke Chill Player in your put.io account settings.
